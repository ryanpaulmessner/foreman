## Question

Board-launched coordinators are named by the epic id, but coordinators registered from the CLI (coord, a coordinator started by hand) carry no epic (research fm-3fm.1). Decide how ownership is set: the coordinator declares its epics when it registers (a flag), the board records it from the first dispatch under that epic, Ryan sets it on the plan, or a mix; and what happens when two live coordinators claim one initiative. Also decide whether the board adds a claim so only one coordinator can take a ticket from a start note, or whether the stage check (launch only if queued) is enough.
