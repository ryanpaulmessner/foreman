## Question

An initiative in the top N can be stuck in ways other than "build ticket ready": only blocked tickets, an open decision ticket that needs Ryan, a draft spec not yet approved, a ticket in review or waiting on a push card, or nothing left. For each case decide: does it count as "moving", does the board nudge, nudge whom with what, or does the next initiative below the line move up into the top N instead.
